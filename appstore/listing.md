### Description (4000 max)
```
Medoly helps you take your medication on time — and remember whether you already did.

It is built for people on long-term treatment: blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol, or any prescription that has to be taken consistently. Large text, few steps, usable by older patients.

REMINDERS THAT ARRIVE
• Set the times for each medicine; they repeat every day
• Reminders work with no internet — the schedule lives on your phone
• Tap the notification to confirm the dose; no hunting through the app

SEE HOW YOU ARE DOING
• A 7-day adherence ring shows at a glance whether you are keeping up
• A 30-day log of which days were complete and which were missed
• Remaining pills are counted down, with a warning before you run out

SCAN A PRESCRIPTION
• Photograph the prescription and the app reads the medicines, doses and frequency
• You always review and correct it before saving — the AI only helps with typing
• No prescription to hand? Entering it manually takes a few taps

LET YOUR DOCTOR FOLLOW ALONG
• Enter the pairing code your doctor gives you to share your adherence
• Your doctor sees adherence, current medicines and upcoming appointments
• Entirely voluntary — disconnect at any time

YOUR DATA SURVIVES A NEW PHONE
• Everything is stored on your device and backed up to your account
• Sign in on a new phone and your prescriptions and history come back

BILINGUAL
• Vietnamese and English, switchable any time in Settings

PRIVACY
• We do not sell your data or use health information for advertising
• No location, no contacts, no tracking across other apps
• Delete your account from inside the app — server data goes with it

IMPORTANT
Medoly provides reminders and record-keeping only. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace your doctor or pharmacist. Always follow your healthcare professional's instructions. Notifications depend on your phone's settings, so do not rely on this app as your only way of remembering medication.

Terms: https://medremind-backend-production.up.railway.app/terms
Privacy: https://medremind-backend-production.up.railway.app/privacy
```
